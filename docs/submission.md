# The capstone submission

How the course asked for the capstone to be submitted (its e-mail to learners, received 26 Nov 2026), and how
FinSight meets it.

## The course's guidelines

1. **Public links:** every deliverable in a cloud storage folder (Google Drive, OneDrive ...), shared as "Anyone with
   the link can view", with downloading allowed. A restricted or inaccessible link may affect the evaluation.
2. **One PDF** that lists the links to all the deliverables.
3. **Upload** the PDF on the upGrad LMS, module *Production Deployment & Industry Capstone*, asset *Capstone
   Submission*. One team member submits; the submission is then visible to the whole team.

## The timeline

| Date | What |
|---|---|
| 27 Nov 2026, 11:59 PM IST | the final deadline; no extensions; only submissions before it can be nominated for the top 10 |
| 5 Dec 2026 | no live session |
| 6 Dec 2026 | the top 10 nominated teams are told |
| 12 Dec 2026 | the final cohort closure session for all learners, and two panel discussions for the top 10 teams (five each) |

So the 10-minute demo is submitted as a recording (W3-14), and a live run is for the panel, if the team is
nominated.

## Our submission

The LMS page asks for one file, the technical report as a PDF, with the links to the demo video, the repository and the trace dashboard inside it and the model-card information in it. `docs/report/final_report.pdf` carries them on its front page and in Appendix E, and §10 is the Responsible AI section and model card. The shared folder: [FinSight_Capstone_HexAgents_Squad](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1dVHhpvG2hf91wKdYiojcEK7JCuTbIXdC).
