3. The stronger critic (W3-03): deterministic checks, no model call
===================================================================
- Metric vocabulary: deposits, noninterest expense, current assets and liabilities, segment figures, net sales vs
  total revenue, the medical cost ratio (named_metrics found no metric in some questions and checked nothing).
- Operation rule: every fetched metric a calculation uses is named by the question or is its implied denominator
  (a ratio passed as soon as its revenue denominator was named).
- Substitution rule: a different metric is either named as such in the answer, or the draft goes back.
- "Not reported" check: flagged when a fetched fact or cited passage holds the asked metric.
- Period and duration: the stated period (fiscal year, quarter; 3 vs 12 months) matches the question and the values.
- Summary check: a "what drove X" answer names X and cites the asked filing's MD&A.
- Refusal checks: a partial refusal answers its factual part; the rule follows the spec's priority (R2 before R3).
Every check has passing and failing tests and a mutation check, flags no correct development answer, and reports
its catch rate on dev-v2's seeded failures (target at least 80 %). The ablation without the critic (W3-01) measures
what it adds.

4. Optimization (W3-07): time, cost, code, and the team's time
==============================================================
- Latency and cost: profile time and tokens per node and tool from the A1 runs and traces; trim the composer's
  evidence to what it can cite; skip the composer's re-ask when no figure is missing; warm the index and clients at
  API start-up; show the answer-cache hit rate. Target: median <= 5.0 s and <= 3,000 tokens per question (A1's), no
  accuracy loss; any prompt change re-pinned and re-measured on the development set.
- Code: dead code removed (e.g. the unused AgentState.messages), the longest functions simplified, docstrings
  current, ruff clean, test coverage of agent/, core/ and api/ reported.
- Test time: the corpus scans (about 8 minutes) behind an opt-in marker, the default suite under 2 minutes.
- The team's time: a 15-minute stand-up at 10:00 each day; the accuracy and optimization PRs merged by Tue 14:00 so
  A5 can run on Tuesday evening; reviews within 3 hours; the report written in parallel from Monday.
