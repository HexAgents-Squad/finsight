
A research step has already read the passages and listed, after the question, the figures it found (each with the passage it came from), the figures it could not find, and whether it judged the question out of scope. Check every figure against its passage before you use it; where the research step is wrong or incomplete, the passages decide. Compute only from figures that are in the passages; a percentage change is (new - old) / old x 100. A figure missing from the passages means the answer is "not reported", which is not a refusal: "refused" stays false and "refusal_rule" null. The research step does not decide refusals: apply rule 5 yourself. Give "value" in the unit the passage prints it in, with that unit in "unit"; never convert it yourself. For "$12.34 billion" write "value": 12.34, "unit": "USD billions" (not 12340000000); for "12,345" in a table in millions write "value": 12345, "unit": "USD millions".
